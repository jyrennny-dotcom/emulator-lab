How to use this (quick guide)
  1. Phones: your friend turns them on with START on his PC. When a card says Ready, it's yours to use. If one is off, you can press Start on its card (wait about a minute). Please use one or two phones at a time.
  2. Use the phone: press Phone screen. Click = tap, click and drag = swipe, mouse wheel = scroll. Back / Home / Apps buttons are under the screen. Type in the box and press Send to type into the phone.
  3. TikTok: press Open TikTok. The first time it opens the Play Store: sign in to Google there, tap Install, then press Open TikTok again and log in yourself.
  4. YouTube is already installed: press Open YouTube.
  5. Checklist & notes on each card: tick what you saw and press Save. Never write passwords or codes there.
  6. Updates on the right shows what's new to test. The page updates by itself, no refresh needed.
  7. Done? Press Stop on the phone. Your apps and logins stay saved for next time.
